Abstract

I train and fine-tune domain-specific models — vision, vision-language, audio and language — and get them running where the work actually happens: on TPU clusters during training, and on small, constrained hardware in deployment. Alongside the modelling I build the software around it: agent systems, gateways, payment backends. The thread through all of it is turning a messy field problem into data, labels, evaluations and a model that keeps working after it ships.
